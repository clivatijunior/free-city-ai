# Free City — Constitutional Kernel

## 1. Non-Aggression Principle (PNA)

The base institutional rule is the Non-Aggression Principle: initiation of force, dispossession,
fraud or coercive interference with another participant or their legitimately owned assets is treated
as a violation.

The simulator does **not** assume all agents will obey this norm.

## 2. Private property

Scarce assets have identifiable owners. Ownership can change through voluntary transfer, gift,
contract, inheritance or another explicitly modeled consensual mechanism.

## 3. Voluntary contracts

Obligations arise from consent. An AI-generated sentence alone never creates a binding obligation.

## 4. Monetary base

The reference currency is FC-BTC and is denominated internally in satoshis.

- 1 FC-BTC = 100,000,000 satoshis.
- Maximum monetary supply = 21,000,000 FC-BTC.
- LLM agents have no authority to mint currency or edit balances.

This is a Bitcoin-inspired monetary model, not a real Bitcoin blockchain.

## 5. No central sovereign

The base layer contains no compulsory taxation authority, central bank or monopoly court.

Voluntary associations, cooperatives, companies, mutual-aid organizations, condominiums,
arbitration providers and other institutions may emerge.

## 6. Experimental neutrality

The simulator must not program favorable results for anarcho-capitalism.

It encodes institutional rules and records what follows from them. Failure modes, concentration,
fraud, free-rider problems and other adverse outcomes must remain observable.

## 7. Separation between cognition and authority

LLMs may propose actions. Only deterministic world services are authoritative over money,
property, contracts and other scarce state.
